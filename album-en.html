Centering

Upload a flat photo of the card. Drag the guides if the auto-detect is off.

Photograph or upload the front of a card. Green lines are the outer edges, gold lines are the inner frame. Left/right and top/bottom are the border split.

Centering-only estimate, not surface, corners, or edges, and not an official PSA, BGS, CGC, or TAG grade. Graders also look at the physical card. Back centering is required only for cards that might be worth grading; the combined prediction uses each company’s front and back tolerances (PSA and CGC are more lenient on the back; BGS is stricter — a BGS 10 needs about 50/50 on the front and 55/45 on the back).
